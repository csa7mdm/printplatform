# PrintPlatform — Terms of Service (Draft)

**Effective:** [date] · Review with Egyptian counsel before launch.

## 1. The Service
PrintPlatform connects customers who need 3D printing with vetted printer owners
and operators who fulfil and deliver orders. PrintPlatform acts as the merchant
of record for customer payments and settles printer owners.

## 2. Accounts & eligibility
You must be 18+ and provide accurate information. Printer owners must complete
verification (including national ID) before receiving jobs. Keep credentials secure.

## 3. Orders & pricing
A quote is an estimate valid for the stated period. An order is formed when you
accept a quote and (where applicable) pay. Pricing is in Egyptian Pounds (EGP).
Quotes are derived from automatic geometry analysis; if analysis fails, an
operator will quote manually.

## 4. Payment
Supported methods include card (Paymob), InstaPay, Vodafone Cash, and cash on
delivery, as offered at checkout. Payment processing is handled by licensed
providers.

## 5. Delivery & quality
We run quality control before dispatch and use third-party couriers (e.g. Bosta).
Risk of loss/damage passes on delivery. Report defects within [X] days; where the
defect is our error we reprint or refund at our discretion.

## 6. Cancellations & refunds
You may cancel before production starts. Refunds are issued to the original
payment method and may take [X] business days.

## 7. Printer owner terms
Owners agree to accept/decline jobs within 3 hours, print to provided specs,
upload completion photos, meet acceptance/QC thresholds, and are paid weekly via
InstaPay or bank transfer minus the platform commission (15–25%).

## 8. Acceptable use
Do not upload unlawful, infringing, or dangerous content (e.g. weapons under
Egyptian law). We may refuse or remove content at our discretion.

## 9. Liability
To the maximum extent permitted by law, PrintPlatform's liability is limited to
the amount paid for the relevant order. We are not liable for indirect damages.
