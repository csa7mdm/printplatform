# PrintPlatform — Privacy Policy (Draft)

**Effective:** [date] · **Applies to:** the PrintPlatform website, customer app,
operator app, and printer-owner app (collectively, "the Service").

This is a starter policy. Review it with a licensed Egyptian counsel before launch.

## 1. Who we are
PrintPlatform operates a 3D-printing fulfilment marketplace in Egypt connecting
customers, printer owners, and operators. For privacy requests, contact
[privacy@your-domain.eg].

## 2. Data we collect
- **Customer:** name (Arabic/English), email, phone, delivery address, payment
  method, uploaded 3D model files (STL/3MF/OBJ), order and quote history.
- **Printer owner:** name, phone, national ID (encrypted at rest), business name,
  bank/InstaPay payout details, printer and material data.
- **Operator:** name, email, login and audit activity.
- **Technical:** IP address, device/browser, logs (via Serilog), usage analytics.

## 3. How we use it
To fulfil and deliver print orders, process payments and payouts, verify identity,
run quality control, provide support, prevent fraud, and comply with law.

## 4. Legal bases (Egypt)
Processing is based on (a) contract performance (fulfilling orders), (b) your
consent (marketing, optional), and (c) legal obligation (tax/anti-fraud records).
Egypt does not yet have a GDPR-equivalent general privacy law, but Law 151/2020
on Personal Data Protection applies to personal data processing.

## 5. Sharing
We share data only with service providers necessary to operate the Service:
payment gateways (Paymob), delivery couriers (Bosta), WhatsApp (notifications),
and hosting/storage providers (MinIO/S3). We do not sell personal data.

## 6. Retention
Order, payment, and payout records are retained for the period required by
Egyptian tax law (typically 5 years). Uploaded model files are retained while
relevant to an order and may be deleted on request or after a retention window.

## 7. Your rights
You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data, subject to
legal retention obligations. Contact [privacy@your-domain.eg].

## 8. Security
National IDs and payout details are encrypted at rest; access is role-restricted.
Payment card data is handled by Paymob (we never store full card numbers).

## 9. Cookies & analytics
The Service may use essential cookies and anonymised analytics. A cookie banner
is required if you add marketing/analytics trackers.

## 10. Changes
We will post material changes here and, where required, notify active users.
